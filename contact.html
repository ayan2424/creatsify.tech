FAQ

Frequently Asked

Quick answers to common questions about working with us

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. A typical motion graphics project takes 2-4 weeks, while full 3D animation projects can range from 6-12 weeks. For urgent projects, we offer rush delivery options at an additional cost. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

We offer custom quotes based on project requirements. Factors include animation style, duration, complexity, number of revisions, and timeline. We're happy to work within various budgets and can suggest options that match your needs. After our initial call, you'll receive a detailed proposal with transparent pricing.

Our standard packages include 2-3 rounds of revisions at key milestones (storyboard, animatic, and final animation). We believe in collaborative refinement to ensure the final product exceeds your expectations. Additional revision rounds can be added if needed.

Absolutely! We work with clients worldwide across different time zones. We use video calls (Zoom, Google Meet), screen sharing, and project management tools like Frame.io for seamless communication and feedback. Most of our clients are remote, and we've perfected our collaboration process.

We deliver final animations in all required formats (MP4, MOV, GIF, WebM, etc.) optimized for your intended platforms—whether it's web, social media, broadcast, or cinema. Source files (After Effects, Blender, Maya projects) can be provided upon request for an additional fee.

Yes, we're happy to sign NDAs and confidentiality agreements to protect your intellectual property. We take client confidentiality very seriously—your ideas and business information are safe with us. Just let us know your requirements before we begin.

We typically work with a 50% deposit to begin work, with the remaining 50% due upon final delivery. For larger projects, we can arrange milestone-based payments. We accept credit cards, PayPal, bank transfers, and wire transfers for international clients.

Definitely! We maintain open communication throughout the project. You'll receive regular updates at key milestones: concept/storyboard, animatic/preview, and near-final render. We use Frame.io for easy review and timestamped feedback on animations.
